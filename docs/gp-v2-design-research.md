# V2 workflow design research

Public visuals inspected on October 21, 2026:

- [Chronograph GP overview](https://www.chronograph.pe/general-partners/): actual public [data collection interface](https://www.chronograph.pe/wp-content/uploads/Flexibility-Out-of-the-Box-Chronograph-1200x750.gif) and [financial-summary interface](https://www.chronograph.pe/wp-content/uploads/Made-for-Todays-Investor-Chronograph.gif). Local research screenshots are retained under `artifacts/gp-v2/`; no proprietary artwork is included in the application.
- [eFront Portfolio Monitoring](https://www.efront.com/en/alternative-investment-software/efront-portfolio-monitoring): reviewed its public workflow presentation for context, rather than adding its product scope.

Applied design choices: compact KPI strip, persistent navigation, clearly labeled reporting frequency, dense readable comparison tables, restrained visual accents, and a direct path from an exception to its supporting source. The actual-versus-budget small multiples use independent company-period axes with zero baselines. Scope is prepared assessment review and an auditable Excel handoff alongside an existing reporting process. No fund accounting, valuation, return modeling, external integration or proprietary capabilities are implied.
